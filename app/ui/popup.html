Safety & operation details
No changes applied.

Independent watchdog: 10-second lease renewed by the native worker every 2 seconds. Preview always restores after 15 seconds. External color changes stop operation and restore saved originals; this is not backlight brightness.

HDR / ICC / Night Light, driver latency, recycled display IDs, hotplug and hardware cursor behavior can limit results. Recovery is best effort if the OS or both processes fail. API/readback do not prove visible effect.
